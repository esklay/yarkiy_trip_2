Ancient Wisdom That Was Waiting for You

The Flower of Life is not just an excursion tour. It is a deep transformation through resonance with the most ancient places of power on Earth.

On the walls of the underground Osireion temple in Abydos, one of humanity's oldest and most mysterious symbols is carved — the Flower of Life. It is etched into granite by an unknown method at the molecular level.

We journey to Egypt to walk the path of ancient initiations: from unconditional love in the Temple of Hathor to deep rebirth in mystical Abydos.

This is a journey of inner transformation — where ancient stones become a mirror, and sacred geometry becomes the language of the soul.

When your body and mind enter the harmonic frequency of Egypt's temples, a natural process of self-restoration and activation of your spiritual potential begins.

This retreat is for those who are ready for deep inner work and seek not tourism, but initiation.

Six Gifts of the Retreat

✔ Places of Power: Ancient temples and pyramids as points of awakening.
✔ Morning Yoga: Asanas and pranayamas under the Egyptian dawn.
✔ Sound Practices: Voice, mantras, vibrations — tuning the vessel.
✔ Dance Therapy: Free movement as the body's prayer.
✔ Art Practice: Creating your own Flower of Life painting.
✔ Group Support: A warm circle of women and a guide by your side.

Eight Days of the Journey

Welcoming participants in Luxor. Check-in at a comfortable hotel on the banks of the Nile. Evening opening circle, introduction to the retreat space, and formation of personal intention for the journey.

Early departure to Dendera. Exploring the breathtaking temple complex of Hathor, goddess of love, music, and motherhood. Visiting the underground crypts and practicing heart-center activation beneath the ancient zodiac ceilings.

Transfer to Abydos — Egypt's most ancient spiritual center. Immersion in the atmosphere of the Temple of Seti I with its unique bas-reliefs. Visiting the underground Osireion, where the Flower of Life is carved. Practice of attuning to the matrix of creation.

Exploring the Luxor Temple, built according to the laws of sacred proportion of the human body. Evening meditation in the glow of the colonnades' nighttime illumination.

Visiting the majestic Karnak complex. Meditative practice in the closed sanctuary of the lion-headed goddess Sekhmet — transforming inner blocks into power.

Exploring the west bank of the Nile, the tombs of the Valley of the Kings, and the Temple of Hatshepsut. Practice of grounding, integrating received knowledge, and deep relaxation.

Morning integration practice, recording retreat results. Closing circle of gratitude. Transfer to the airport.

Returning home with a new inner resource and a deep understanding of your sacred nature.
